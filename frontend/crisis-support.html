In a Crisis?

Know the signs and act fast.

Recognize Signs

Severe mood swings, talk of death or suicide, hopelessness, withdrawing from others.

Stay Calm

Breathe deeply. Your calm presence helps them feel safe and supported.

Listen Without Judgment

Let them speak. Don't interrupt or offer solutions — just listen with compassion.

Remove Means

If there's immediate danger, remove access to weapons, medications, or other harmful objects.

Stay Connected

Don't leave them alone. Stay with them until professional help arrives.

Follow Up

Check in with them regularly. Ongoing support shows you care and helps prevent relapse.
